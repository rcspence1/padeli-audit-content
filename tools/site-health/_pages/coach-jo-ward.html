About Jo

Jo Ward is the LTA’s Head of Coach Education and Qualifications and a co-founder and director of WimX Padel, the coaching team in residence at The Heron Padel Club in Brentwood. She has represented Great Britain at FIP Senior World and European Padel Championships and sits among the top of the GB LTA Open Padel rankings. Her coaching draws on teaching technique, tactics, physiology and psychology.

Career highlights

  • LTA Head of Coach Education and Qualifications
  • LTA Master Performance Coach (Level 5) and Padel Tutor
  • Co-founder and Director of WimX Padel (resident coaching team at The Heron Padel Club)
  • Represented Great Britain at the 2024 FIP Seniors World Padel Championships (Women’s 40+)
  • Captained GB Super Seniors at the FIP Senior European Team Championships, Alicante (2023)
  • Top-ranked in GB LTA Open Padel rankings
  • Led LTA’s national padel coaching pathway build-out (Level 2 to Level 5) including Coach Development Centres
